# LSDKChat help

## What is LSDKChat
LSDKChat is a small real-time chat app that runs in your web browser (no download). You can send messages, photos, files and voice notes, post status updates, and make voice and video calls, one-to-one or in groups. It is free. It is made by Lungelo Mthethwa (operator of the service). Open it from the link you were given and sign in; on a phone you can add it to your home screen from the browser menu.

## Creating an account and signing in
Tap Register, choose a username (3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers and underscore only), enter your email and a password, and tick both consent boxes (Terms and Privacy). You must be 18 or older. Confirm your email using the link we send you, then sign in. If you forget your password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and open the link in the email. If the confirmation or reset email does not arrive, check spam and wait a few minutes: the free email sender only allows a few emails per hour.

## Starting a chat
Tap the pencil button (✎) for a new direct chat and pick a person, or the people button (👥) for a new group. A group needs a name (1 to 60 characters) and can have up to 50 members including you. The 📝 button opens "Notes to myself", a private chat only you can see.

## Groups: admins and members
Group admins can rename the group, add or remove members, and make other members admins. A group always needs at least one admin. Admins can delete any message in the group. You can leave a group from Chat info. Direct chats cannot be left and cannot have extra members.

## Sending messages
Type and press send. A message can be up to 1000 characters. Hold a message (or right-click, or press Enter on it with the keyboard) to see its actions: reply, react with an emoji, edit or delete your own message, save it, set a reminder, or report it. You can see "typing…" when someone is writing and "Seen" when your message was read (if they allow read receipts).

## Photos, files and voice notes
Use the paperclip to attach a photo, video, audio file or document. Files are limited to 20 MB and only some file types are allowed. Press the microphone to record a voice note (up to 5 minutes) and press the arrow to send. You can also drag a file onto the conversation on a computer. The 🗣 button dictates text by voice (browser feature; English and Afrikaans usually work best).

## Status updates (stories)
In the Status tab you can post text, a photo, a video or audio. Status updates last 24 hours and are visible to people who share a chat with you. You can see who viewed them, and people can like and comment. Turn sharing off in Settings > Privacy if you do not want to share status updates.

## Voice and video calls (one-to-one)
Open a direct chat and tap 📞 (voice) or 🎥 (video). You can call someone once you have both sent each other at least one message. The other person's phone rings only while LSDKChat is open in their browser; there are no push notifications yet, so calls can be missed. Allow microphone and camera access when the browser asks. You can mute, turn the camera off, and end the call. The Calls tab shows your call history.

## Group calls
Open a group chat and tap 📞 or 🎥. Every member is rung and can accept, decline, or join later while the call is running (a "Join" bar appears in the group). Up to 6 people can be in a group call at the same time, and video works best with up to 4 people. If everyone leaves, the call ends. If a call is already running in the group, tapping the call button joins it.

## Calls do not connect or no sound
Check that the browser is allowed to use the microphone and camera (look at the address bar). Both people must keep LSDKChat open. Some strict networks (school, office or some mobile networks) block direct calls; the app uses a relay to help, but a very restrictive network may still fail. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, close other apps using the camera, and refresh the page with Ctrl+F5 (on a phone, close the tab and open it again). If someone is already on another call, you will see that they are busy.

## Tools page: search, library, saved, reminders, scheduled
Tap the 🧰 button. Search finds messages, files and links across all your chats (type at least 2 letters). Library sorts photos, videos, voice notes, music, documents and links automatically. Saved shows messages you saved into collections. Reminders lists your message reminders. Scheduled lists messages waiting to be sent. "Open in chat" jumps to the original message.

## Send later (schedule a message), reminders and saving messages
To send a message later, write it, press 🗓 and pick a time (text only; up to 30 waiting messages). You can also choose to send when the person comes online in a direct chat. Hold a message and choose "Remind me…" to get a pop-up reminder (it appears while the app is open, or next time you open it). Hold a message and choose "Save message…" to keep it in a collection. Press and hold a chat in the list to mark it as unread.

## Settings overview
Open Settings from the avatar in the top corner. It has Account, Privacy, Appearance, Notifications, Ringtones, Calls, Accessibility and Legal sections.

## Privacy settings: online status, last seen, read receipts
In Settings > Privacy you control whether others can see when you are online and your last seen time, whether read receipts ("Seen") are sent, and whether your status updates are shared with people you chat with. If you turn something off you may also stop seeing the same information from others.

## Appearance: themes, dark mode and chat backgrounds
In Settings > Appearance you can choose a light or dark theme (dark mode), pick a chat background, or upload your own picture as a background. A background you upload is saved only on that device, so you need to set it again on another device.

## Ringtones
In Settings > Ringtones you can choose the default ringtone for calls, set a different ringtone for a specific person, preview tones, and add your own tones (up to 5 custom tones, each up to 3 MB and 30 seconds). Custom tones are stored on that device only.

## Notifications and sounds
Settings > Notifications has the message sound and other alerts. Sounds and pop-ups only work while LSDKChat is open in a browser tab; there are no push notifications yet.

## Accessibility, text size and dictation
In Settings > Accessibility you can make the text bigger or smaller by choosing one of five text sizes (font size), turn on high contrast, reduce motion, switch screen-reader announcements on or off, and set the dictation language. Tab to a message and press Enter to open its actions. The 🗣 button in a chat dictates your message; say "send", "clear" or "stop" as voice commands (English only).

## Profile
Open your profile from the avatar in the top corner (or Settings). You can change your profile picture (up to 15 MB), username and bio (up to 160 characters), and your password. "Download my data" exports your account details, messages you sent, status updates and call history. You can also sign out or delete your account. Deleting your account is permanent.

## Privacy, security and encryption
LSDKChat is not end-to-end encrypted: messages and files are encrypted on their way to and from the server, but the person who runs the service can technically read stored messages (they only look at content to review abuse reports, security problems or legal requests). Do not share passwords or highly sensitive information. Calls go directly between devices (or through an encrypted relay when needed) and are never recorded or stored; the call list stores who called whom, when, and how long, for 90 days. Everyone must accept the current Terms and Privacy Policy; if they change you will be asked to accept again. Data is stored in the eu-west-2 region.

## Reporting abuse and blocking problems
Hold a message and choose Report, add a short reason, and the operator reviews it. You can also report a status update or a comment. Harassment, unwanted or sexual messages and calls are not allowed under the Terms.

## Known limits and offline use
LSDKChat needs an internet connection; it does not work offline. Reminders, ringtones and calls only work while the app is open in a browser tab; there are no push notifications yet. Dictation depends on your browser's speech service. Voice notes and videos have no captions yet. Scheduled messages are text only. Saved messages and reminders disappear if the original message is deleted.

## Messages not appearing or the app looks old
Check your internet connection (a banner appears if the live connection is down). Refresh with Ctrl+F5, or on a phone close the tab and open it again. If the site shows a 404 page, the operator is fixing the site; try again later.

## Contact and support
For problems with the app, contact the operator, Lungelo Mthethwa, using the contact email on the Terms and Privacy pages (Settings > Legal).

## About LUNEX
LUNEX is the assistant inside LSDKChat. It can answer questions about how to use the app, research a topic on the web and give a short sourced answer, summarize a chat, an article link or pasted text, translate or rewrite text, do quick calculations and tell the time. Type /help to see the commands. LUNEX is an AI and can make mistakes, so double-check anything important. It only sees what you send to it; it cannot read your other chats unless you choose to share them with it.
